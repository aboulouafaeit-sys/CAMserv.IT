# CAMserv Master Risk Register

| Risk ID | Title | Probability | Impact | Score | Owner | Root Cause | Mitigation & Contingency Strategy | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **RSK-SEC-01** | Unauthorized Camera Credential Exposure | LOW | CRITICAL | **HIGH** | Cybersecurity Eng | Hardcoded or unencrypted RTSP passwords in config files. | Implement AES-256-GCM encrypted credential vault with master key stored in environment or OS secret manager. Zero plaintext logging. | **MITIGATED** |
| **RSK-SEC-02** | Evidence Tampering or Chain Break | LOW | CRITICAL | **HIGH** | Forensics Eng | Malicious administrator editing or deleting video evidence files on disk. | Continuous SHA-256 hash chaining from genesis + Ed25519 digital signatures on evidence manifests + independent verification CLI. | **ACTIVE** |
| **RSK-PRV-01** | Re-Identification via Biometric Infiltration | LOW | CRITICAL | **HIGH** | Privacy Eng | Accidental embedding of facial recognition vectors into vector database. | Strictly enforce non-biometric schema in Milvus. Detections only output generic class boxes. Face and body blurring prior to vectorization or storage. | **MITIGATED** |
| **RSK-OPS-01** | Frame Queue Memory Exhaustion | MEDIUM | HIGH | **HIGH** | Pipeline Eng | Network congestion or slow inference causing unbounded queue buildup. | BackpressureQueue with strict capacity limits and `drop_oldest` policy on raw frames; memory watchdog drops processing before OOM killer trips. | **MITIGATED** |
| **RSK-HW-01** | GPU Driver / CUDA Incompatibility | MEDIUM | HIGH | **HIGH** | Platform Eng | Server without NVIDIA GPU or mismatched CUDA drivers crashing the pipeline. | DetectorFactory implements dynamic fallback chain: TensorRT -> ONNX GPU -> ONNX CPU -> Mock/CPU fallback. Automated hardware detection at startup. | **MITIGATED** |
| **RSK-NET-01** | Camera Stream Stalls / Drops | HIGH | MEDIUM | **MEDIUM** | NVR Eng | Flaky RTSP connections or network drops causing hung decoder threads. | Exponential backoff auto-reconnect in CameraStream with non-blocking timeouts and synthetic heartbeat frames. | **MITIGATED** |
| **RSK-PRD-01** | Lack of Unified Web Interface | HIGH | HIGH | **HIGH** | Frontend Eng | Enterprise users unable to operate the platform without writing custom API clients. | Build embedded single-page CAMserv Enterprise Web Console covering all 12 operational screens. | **IN_PROGRESS** |
| **RSK-LGL-01** | EU AI Act High-Risk Classification Penalty | LOW | CRITICAL | **HIGH** | Compliance Eng | Deploying surveillance AI in EU public spaces without mandatory conformity assessments. | Publish comprehensive conformity evaluation, DPIA templates, audit logs, and mandatory Human-in-the-Loop gates in `docs/PRIVACY.md`. | **MITIGATED** |
