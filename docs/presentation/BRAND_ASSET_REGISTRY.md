## 1. Executive Brand Identity Overview

The official visual identity of **CAMserv** represents industrial security engineering, mathematical precision, non-biometric privacy preservation, and sovereign edge computing.

### The Cybernetic Sentinel Crest
The core visual mark synthesizes:
1. **The Falcon Crest:** High-altitude situational awareness and continuous oversight.
2. **The Optical Aperture Iris:** High-definition video stream decoding, calibration, and spatial analysis.
3. **Neural Circuitry:** Low-latency on-device inference, kinematic tracking, and graph reasoning.
4. **The Defensive Shield:** Tamper-evident cryptographic proof (AES-256-GCM, SHA-256 hash chains, Ed25519) and strict GDPR fail-closed privacy protection.

## 4. Typography Standards

* **Brand Wordmark & Display Titles:** Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Segoe UI, sans-serif.
  - Case: `CAM` (all-caps, white, font-weight 900) + `serv` (lowercase, cyan, font-weight 900).
* **Technical Telemetry & Evidence Data:** JetBrains Mono, Consolas, Courier New, monospace.
  - Tabular figure layout (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) must be used for coordinates, SHA-256 hashes, timestamps, and confidence percentages.

## 5. Usage & Integrity Rules

1. **Clear Space:** Maintain an exclusion perimeter equal to at least 25% of the emblem's width around all sides.
2. **Aspect Ratio Preservation:** Never stretch, distort, or compress the emblem.
3. **No Decorative Clutter:** Do not add dropshadows, rainbow gradients, or cartoon elements to the mark.
4. **Authenticity:** The official mark generated for CAMserv is protected. Do not replace it with arbitrary AI clip-art.
